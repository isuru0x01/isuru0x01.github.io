### Research Horizons

Our research group is actively pursuing key frontiers in applied computing:

1. **Safety-Critical Computer Vision**: Developing edge-deployable multi-modal models combining YOLOv8, Vision-Language Models (CLIP, LLaVA), and IoT sensor fusion for real-time hazard and fire recognition in industrial facilities.
2. **Generative AI in Computing Pedagogy**: Conducting empirical mixed-methods studies assessing the cognitive impact, code fluency, and assessment strategies surrounding AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT) among novice programmers.
3. **Low-Resource Language Technologies**: Releasing open-source datasets and text processing utilities for low-resource South Asian languages, notably our [10-million-row Sinhala narrative corpus on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21194859).
