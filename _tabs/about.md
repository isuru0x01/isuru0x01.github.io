## Academic Profile

I am a **Computer Science Educator and Applied AI Researcher** with over five years of higher education experience across the UK and Sri Lanka, currently serving as a **Senior Lecturer in the Faculty of Information Technology at Horizon Campus**. 

My teaching and research portfolio spans **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and Software Engineering**, with a steadfast emphasis on project-based learning, research-led supervision, and industry-aligned curriculum design.

My research centers on **multi-modal deep learning and computer vision** for safety-critical and sustainability-oriented applications, notably real-time hazard detection in industrial environments. My current work integrates YOLO-family architectures, vision–language models (such as CLIP and LLaVA), and IoT sensor streams for robust early warning and monitoring systems. In parallel, a complementary research strand investigates the **pedagogical implications of AI coding assistants** for novice computer science students through mixed-methods studies evaluating learning outcomes, code quality, and learner autonomy.

Prior to academia, I accumulated extensive industry experience in software engineering, cloud systems deployment, and **DevSecOps**, which directly informs my hands-on, deployment-oriented approach to academic research, curriculum design, and student mentorship.

---

## Research Focus Areas

- **AI Applications in Education**: Investigating the impact of generative AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT), prompt engineering methodologies, and personalized learning systems in higher education computing curricula.
- **Applied Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision**: Developing edge-deployable deep learning and computer vision architectures (YOLOv8, R-CNN, Vision-Language Models) for hazard detection, industrial safety, and predictive analytics.
- **Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics**: Enterprise security risk assessments, automated vulnerability remediation, DevSecOps pipeline security, and digital forensic investigative frameworks.
- **Low-Resource NLP & Ethical AI**: Building open datasets, corpus collection tools, and language technologies for low-resource languages (Sinhala NLP), alongside ethical considerations in AI deployment.
