## 🔬 Research Supervision & Student Mentorship

I direct the **Applied AI Research Group** at Horizon Campus, supervising over **30 undergraduate capstone projects annually**. My supervision philosophy emphasizes:
- **Publication & Dissemination**: Mentoring undergraduate teams through empirical experimentation, manuscript preparation, and conference presentation. (In 2025 alone, our student research groups produced **8 accepted extended abstracts** at the Horizon Interdisciplinary Research Symposium).
- **Production-Ready Engineering**: Encouraging students to deploy functional prototypes, build reproducible GitHub repositories, and release open-source datasets or software.
- **Ethical & Safety Awareness**: Guiding students to consider data privacy, algorithmic fairness, and safety impacts in AI design.
